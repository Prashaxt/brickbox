brickbox 0.4.0 test page

Part 1 runs by itself when the page loads. Parts 2 to 10 need your mouse. Serve the repo over http and build first, because this page imports from ../dist/: run npm run build, then npx serve ., then open /demo/test-0.4.0.html.

1. Automatic checks

These press, drag and rebuild real boxes with synthetic pointer events and read the DOM. Timings depend on your machine.

2. pressSink

Press and hold each card. The readout shows the value brickbox sets (--bb-p, the share of depth the box keeps while pressed) and the depth that leaves. Depth is 40px. A card with no pressSink must not sink. 100 and anything above counts as 99. Release to see it come back.

3. shadow

Direction follows light: light from the top-left casts to the bottom-right, from the right casts to the left. The donut's shadow must show through its hole. Hover moves nothing here, so you are looking at the shadows themselves.

Playground




4. Dragging must not select text


Press and drag across the text in each card, including past the card edge. In drag and both, the readout must show userSelect: none while you hold, and no text may highlight. In hover a mouse doesn't drag, so selecting text works normally. Click into an input: it must still take focus and typing in every card.





5. Performance


Builds 30 donuts (about 96 wall strips each) off screen and times it. Identical donuts share one sampling. Different ones each sample their own path. 0.2.0 took about 2.6 s for the identical case and about 2.7 s per update({}) on this kind of page.

7. Colour tracking


The walls follow the face as its colour changes, with no call to update(). Watch the side walls, not only the face. The second card changes on :hover instantly, the third with a 0.4 s transition (the walls should fade along with it), and the last follows a class on <html>.







8. Shapes with calc()


The walls must follow every notch and curve. Before 0.4.0 these fell back to a plain rounded rectangle.





9. Nesting with lift


A small remote. The body tilts with the pointer and rotates when you drag it. The keys are nested boxes with lift: press one and it sinks, the counter goes up, and the body must not drag. Hover over the keys and the body keeps tilting. GO sits on top of OK with lift: 12. Their shadows fall on the body's face.





10. A box far below the screen


Scroll down. The box at the bottom waits to build its walls until you get within about 300px of it. The chip at the bottom right counts its wall strips: 0 before, more than 0 after.
